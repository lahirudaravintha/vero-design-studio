03 Good to Know

Questions, answered before you ask.

A few things clients tend to want clear from the start. Anything we haven't covered, just ask.

What kinds of spaces do you take on?

Luxury residences, hospitality venues and commercial interiors - from full home fit-outs and apartments to offices, showrooms and restaurants. If it's a considered interior, we'd love to hear about it.

Do you design only, or build as well?

Both. We take projects from concept and space planning through to turnkey delivery on site - including bespoke joinery made in our own workshop - so the finished space matches the drawings exactly.

Is the cabinetry really made in-house?

Yes. Our cabinetry and built-in joinery are engineered and finished in-house, which lets us control quality to the millimetre and keep the detailing true to the design.

Where are you based, and how far do you travel?

We're based in Colombo and work on projects across Sri Lanka. For the right project we're happy to discuss work further afield - just ask.

How long does a typical project take?

It depends on scope, but most fit-outs run a few months from brief to handover. We'll give you a realistic programme up front and keep you updated at every stage.

How do we get started?

Reach out via the contact details below or message us on WhatsApp. We'll arrange an initial conversation to understand your space, your brief and how we can help.
