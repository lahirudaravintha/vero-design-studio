About the Studio

A studio built on craft, control and a single coherent idea.

01 Who We Are

VERO Design Studio is a Colombo practice for businesses that take their environments seriously. We treat every fit-out as an extension of the brand - considered in plan, precise in detail, warm in material.

From the first measured drawing to the final installed joinery, the work stays in-house. That control is what lets us hold a space to a single idea - and deliver it turnkey, exactly as drawn. Behind that discipline are two people who lead every project personally.

02 Our Founders

Sachith Senevirathne

Chief Executive Officer

Sachith is a CAPM-certified Site Engineer and Project Manager with a comprehensive background in construction management, structural oversight, and project operations. With over 10 years of experience, he pairs technical engineering acumen with rigorous strategic planning - keeping every project precise, accountable and on programme.

Maheshi Seneviratne

Chief Design Officer

With a strong history in interior design at organisations such as Roux Brothers, Maheshi brings over a decade of experience in crafting ideal living and working spaces. She leads the end-to-end design process, guiding both residential and corporate clients through a seamless journey where ideas are turned into reality through precise execution.
