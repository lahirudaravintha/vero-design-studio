A studio built on craft, control and a single coherent idea.

01 Who We Are

Welcome to Vero Projects, where your vision for a dream space comes to life! Led by a passionate husband-and-wife duo, our team combines 10+ years of civil engineering expertise with the artistry of interior design.

From concept to completion, we work closely with you to tailor spaces that enhance your lifestyle and elevate your environment.

02 Our Founders

Sachith Senevirathne

MBA (UK), B.Eng (Civil Eng)
Chief Executive Officer

With over a decade of experience in project management, engineering, and construction, he brings a unique blend of technical expertise and practical leadership to the interior design industry. His background in delivering complex, high-value projects enables him to bridge the gap between design vision and successful execution. He specializes in translating concepts into buildable solutions, coordinating multidisciplinary teams, managing budgets and timelines, and ensuring quality throughout the project lifecycle. His experience allows him to provide valuable insights into design feasibility, project delivery, construction detailing, and client-focused outcomes across commercial, hospitality, and workplace environments.
